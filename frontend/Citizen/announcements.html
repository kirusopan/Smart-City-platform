Emergency Road Closure

Alert
Jan 18, 2026
URGENT

Highway 101 will be closed for emergency repairs from January 19-21, 2026. The closure is necessary due to structural issues discovered during routine inspection.

Affected Areas
  • Highway 101 between Exit 25 and Exit 32
  • All connecting ramps in the affected section
  • Service roads adjacent to the highway
Alternative Routes

Drivers are advised to use the following alternative routes:

  • Highway 50: Main alternative route with additional lane capacity
  • Route 7: Local road option for shorter distances
  • Interstate 95: For long-distance travelers
Expected Impact

Traffic delays of 15-30 minutes are expected on alternative routes during peak hours. Commuters are advised to:

  • Allow extra travel time
  • Consider carpooling or public transportation
  • Check real-time traffic updates before departure
Updates and Information

For real-time updates on the closure and reopening schedule, please:

  • Visit our traffic information website
  • Follow @CityTraffic on social media
  • Call our traffic hotline: (555) 123-4567
Note: Emergency vehicles will have priority access. If reopening occurs earlier than scheduled, notifications will be sent immediately.
